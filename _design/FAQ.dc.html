Frequently asked questions

Ordering

How long does a poshak take?

Nitya Seva designs ship in 7–10 days, Utsav in 12–15 days and Heirloom pieces in 20–30 days. The delivery date at checkout already includes making time.

Can I order a design exactly as shown?

Yes. Choose the size, colourway and the name to embroider, and pay at checkout. Want changes? Use “Customise this design” to plan it with our team.

When do festival orders close?

We close orders [X] weeks before Janmashtami, Radhashtami and Diwali so every karigar has time. Dates are shown on the site and sent to subscribers.

Custom designs

How does a custom design work?

Send a brief with photos you like. A design advisor calls you, then sends a concept board and an itemised quote. You pay an advance, approve a final sketch and name preview, and stitching begins.

Is the design consultation free?

Yes. You pay only when you approve the quote.

How many changes can I make?

Two sketch revisions are included. Changes after the sketch is signed off are quoted separately.

Size & name

How do I know my Thakurji’s size?

Use the size guide, or send us a photo with a scale and we’ll tell you for free.

Which scripts can you embroider?

Devanagari, English, or both, in gold or silver zari. You approve a preview before we stitch.

Payments & delivery

Do you offer cash on delivery?

No. Each poshak is made specially for you, so we take prepaid orders only: UPI, cards and netbanking.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, to [COUNTRIES]. Courier charges appear at checkout; any import duties are paid by the recipient.

Can I return a poshak?

Personalised items can’t be returned. If it doesn’t fit because of our error, we remake it free. See policies.

Care

How do I care for zari and stone work?

Spot clean or dry clean only. Store folded in the keepsake box with a layer of butter paper, away from moisture.
