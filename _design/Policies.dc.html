Made-to-order & returns

Every DivineKraft poshak is stitched after you order, in your Thakurji’s size and with His name embroidered. Because each piece is personalised, we can’t accept returns or exchanges.

Before stitching starts you approve the name spelling (and, for custom orders, the final sketch). Please check these carefully.

If the poshak doesn’t fit because of our error (for example, we stitched a different size from the one confirmed), we remake it free of charge.
