Operating model · v1 (SLAs and percentages are proposals to validate in the pilot)

Human-led custom design: brief → quote → sketch → stitch

Every custom poshak passes through a person, never a configurator. The customer defines the need, a design advisor shapes it into a priced concept, and production starts only after two approvals: the quote (money) and the final sketch (design).

Rules of the model

  • Payment: [50]% at Gate 1, balance after the QC video and before dispatch. No COD on custom orders.
  • Revisions: 2 sketch revisions free. After that, or a change made after Gate 2, the order is re-quoted.
  • Cancellation: full refund before Gate 2. After the sketch is signed off, the advance covers materials and karigar time.
  • Name accuracy: the spelling is locked only after the customer replies “Approved” to the preview.
  • Size: a free redo if the size is wrong because of our error. The customer must give a size photo with a scale.
  • Festival cut-off: briefs close [X] weeks before Janmashtami and Radhashtami, with capped slots for each karigar.
  • Brief → quote conversion: unpaid quotes get one reminder on day 3 and expire on day 7.

Team roles

Design advisor
Front face. Runs consults, writes the spec, sends quotes, converts. Remote is fine.
Designer
Concept boards, sketches, name previews, approves off-rate-card quotes.
Production coordinator
Based in Vrindavan. Assigns karigars, collects progress photos, manages slots.
QC lead
Checks each piece against the signed sketch, shoots the QC video, packs the keepsake box.

Pilot staffing: one person can be advisor + designer; one coordinator can also do QC.
