How to measure

  1. 1Seat Thakurji on a flat surfaceMeasure Him seated, the way He sits on the singhasan.
  2. 2Measure base to crownHold a scale upright from the base to the top of the head, without the mukut.
  3. 3Measure across the shouldersShoulder to shoulder, across the front.
