About DivineKraft

Born in Vrindavan.
Stitched as seva.

In most homes, Thakurji is not an idol. He is family.

So why should His clothes come off a shelf?

DivineKraft began with a simple belief: a family that calls their Laddu Gopal “Lalla” or “Kanha” deserves a poshak made for Him alone. Not picked from a pile, not machine-stitched in bulk, but planned with you and embroidered by hand.

We work with karigar families in Vrindavan whose hands have shaped zardozi, gota-patti and resham for generations. Every poshak is started only after you order, carries the name you call Him, and comes home with the name of the karigar who made it.
